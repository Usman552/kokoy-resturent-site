Through the arch

Step inside

The house

Arched rooms in warm plaster, sculpture on the walls and low light over every table. A menu that runs from the main course to patisserie.

KOKOY is a restaurant and patisserie in Gulgasht Colony, Multan. Come for dinner, stay for dessert, or settle into the lounge with something cold to drink.

The room

Arched windows, sculpted plaster and lamps set low over dark tables.

The plate

Blistered-crust pizza, crisp prawns, salmon and pasta.

The pastry

Patisserie is half of the name. Leave room for cheesecake.

The welcome

Deep lounge chairs and garden views through the glass.

A garden behind glass

A double-height room in warm plaster: cream armchairs at dark tables, palms growing inside a glass court and a ring of light overhead.
